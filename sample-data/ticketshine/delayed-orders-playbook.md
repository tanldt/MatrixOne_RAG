# Delayed Orders Playbook

## First checks

Check the event date and time, order status, seller confirmation, transfer provider status, and any operations notes. Confirm whether the delay is normal for the venue or unusual for the delivery window.

## Customer message for expected delivery

Tell the customer that the order is being actively monitored, that delivery is still expected, and that support will follow up if the delivery window changes.

## When delay becomes urgent

Escalate when the event starts within 24 hours, the seller is unresponsive, the transfer provider reports a failure, or the customer has already missed a committed delivery time.
