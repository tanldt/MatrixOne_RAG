# Ticket Transfer Guide

## Missing transfer email

Check whether the transfer email was generated, whether the recipient email is correct, whether the customer checked spam or promotions folders, and whether the ticket provider has a transfer delay.

## Wrong recipient email

If the email address is wrong, verify ownership of the order, update the recipient email only through the approved workflow, and request a resend from the seller or provider.

## Transfer accepted but tickets not visible

Ask the customer to sign into the provider account directly and check upcoming events. If the provider account shows no tickets, escalate to operations.
