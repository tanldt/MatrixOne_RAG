# Support Escalation SOP

## Escalate to operations

Escalate to operations when seller action is required, the event starts within 24 hours, transfer delivery failed, replacement tickets may be needed, or refund approval depends on fulfillment status.

## Escalation note format

Include order number, event time, customer issue, checks already completed, seller status, and the exact decision needed from operations.

## Do not escalate

Do not escalate simple customer education questions, duplicate requests, or cases where the answer is already available in the support policy.
